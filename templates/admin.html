CampusSphere Role Structure

Admin

Full system access. Admin can manage students, users, complaints, rooms, placements and DBMS features.

Faculty

Academic and operational access. Faculty can manage attendance, examinations, room bookings, placements and complaints.

Student

Student-specific access. Students can view their academic information, attendance, examinations, placements and complaints.

User Management — DBMS Concepts

  • Primary Key: user_id uniquely identifies every user account.
  • Unique Constraint: username must be unique.
  • Foreign Keys: student_id and faculty_id connect authentication records with academic records.
  • Role-Based Access Control: the role column determines which application modules a user can access.
  • Referential Integrity: linked student and faculty records must exist in their respective tables.
