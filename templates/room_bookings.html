DBMS Features Used

The Room Booking module demonstrates database-based resource management.


  • Foreign Keys connect bookings with classrooms and faculty.
  • Transactions can be used to safely create or cancel bookings.
  • Constraints maintain valid classroom and faculty references.
  • View: room_booking_details combines booking, classroom and faculty information.
  • Room availability can be managed through booking status and classroom status.
