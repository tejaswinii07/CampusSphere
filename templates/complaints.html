Complaint Management — DBMS Concepts

  • Primary Key: Each complaint is uniquely identified using complaint_id.
  • Foreign Keys: student_id references the students table and category_id references complaint_categories.
  • Relational Join: Complaint information can be combined with student and category information.
  • Role-Based Access: Students manage their own complaints, while Faculty and Admin can manage complaint status and resolution.
  • Data Integrity: Foreign-key constraints prevent complaints from referencing non-existent students or categories.
