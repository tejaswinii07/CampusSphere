Placement Module

CampusSphere stores company information, placement drives and student applications using separate relational tables.


  • companies stores recruiting company information.
  • placement_drives stores job role, package and eligibility details.
  • placement_applications connects students with placement drives.
  • A unique constraint prevents the same student from applying to the same drive more than once.
  • Placement status can be monitored through the student placement information.
